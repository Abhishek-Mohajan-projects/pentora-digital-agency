1. Introduction

Pentora Digital ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, you can disable cookies through your browser settings.

2. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, improve functionality, and provide a better browsing experience.

Cookies can be "session" cookies (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent" cookies (remain until you delete them or they expire).

3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Remember your preferences (e.g., dark mode)
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns
  • Improve our content and user experience
  • Provide relevant information and offers

4. Types of Cookies We Use

5. Managing Your Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies through settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
  • Cookie Banner: Use our cookie consent banner to accept or decline non-essential cookies.
  • Opt-Out: Disable third-party cookies through your browser preferences.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies on our website:

  • Google Analytics: Tracks page views and usage patterns (anonymized data).
  • Social Media: Embed widgets from LinkedIn, GitHub, Instagram, Facebook.
  • Embedded Content: YouTube videos or other embedded media may set cookies.

We do not control these third-party cookies. Please refer to their respective privacy policies.
